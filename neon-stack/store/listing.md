## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Stack the neon blocks. Chase the perfect drop.

Neon Stack is a satisfying one-tap tower builder. Blocks slide across the screen,
you tap to drop them, and every overhang gets sliced away. Line a block up
perfectly and you keep your full width, build a streak and multiply your score.
Miss, and the run is over - unless you fancy one more go.

CLIMB THE SPIRE
You are not the tower. You are keeping NS-7 alive - a maintenance drone with one
cell bank, a bad attitude and a job to finish. Thirty decks stand between it and
the transmitter that the last colony depends on.

SOMETHING IS ALWAYS IN THE WAY
- Crawlers walk your deck and want your drone. Drop a block on them.
- Turrets sit on the deck and blow up anything that lands on them. Cut them off
the edge instead.
- Wardens guard every chapter finale. Land on them until they fall over.

Getting good is the survival plan: three perfect drops in a row refill a cell.

WHY YOU'LL KEEP PLAYING
- One thumb, one rule: tap to drop.
- 30 decks to clear, each with a goal you can see from the bottom.
- Perfect drops chain into combos and earn stars - chase all 90.
- Daily missions and a daily streak give you a reason to come back tomorrow.
- Unlock six neon themes with the coins you earn from perfect drops.
- Endless mode when you just want to see how high you get.

FEATURES
- Endless one-tap gameplay, perfect for a queue or a coffee break.
- Reflex-based scoring: no timers, no waiting, no energy bars.
- Perfect-drop detection that rewards precision over button mashing.
- A drone that flinches, celebrates and talks to itself on the way up.
- A 21-track soundtrack that reacts to what you do and never loops.
- Your progress, stars and best score are always saved.
- Small download, works offline, plays in portrait.

Whether you have thirty seconds or thirty minutes, the Spire is worth climbing.
How high can you get NS-7?
```
